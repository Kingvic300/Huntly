Find work without handing your data to anyone.

Huntly searches the job sources you choose, ranks every job against your profile, tailors ATS-friendly CVs, and drafts applications you approve. It runs entirely on your computer.

Download

Pick the installer for your computer.

Linux

Debian, Ubuntu and Kali, or any distribution with the AppImage.

macOS

Apple silicon and Intel. macOS 11 or later.

First launch: right-click Huntly in Applications and choose Open.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

If SmartScreen warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Already installed? Huntly updates itself: new versions download in the background and you restart when it suits you.

Your data stays on your computerJobs, CVs and history live in a local folder. There is no Huntly server.
You bring your own keysGoogle, X and Groq credentials are encrypted with your master password.
Nothing is sent without youEvery application email waits for your explicit approval.
No telemetryNo analytics or crash reports. Read the code to check.
